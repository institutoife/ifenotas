# Country flag artwork

The Bolivia, Ecuador and Latin American country SVGs are unmodified Twemoji graphics from [jdecked/twemoji](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji), originally created by Twitter and other contributors.

Graphics licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). National flag assets come from the Twemoji `assets/svg/` collection.
